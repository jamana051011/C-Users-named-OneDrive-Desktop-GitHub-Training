# OVERVIEW

The Configuration Guide outlines how Authenticated Administrators can learn and configure EDID of a particular display to support all of your displays’ features

---

## INTENDED AUDIENCE  

The information in this document is for authorized system administrators.

## SYSTEM SETU

1. Ensure that the KVM’s power is turned off or disconnected from both the unit and the computer.
 Using a USB cable (Type-A to Type-B), connect the PC to the KVM host’s K/M port one. 
3. Connect a USB keyboard and mouse to the two USB console ports.
4. Connect a video cable between the PC and the KVM video 1 port.
5. KVM—Connect a DP display to the KVM console video output connector.
6. Power up the PC and the KVM.
